# LynnaLab Credits
LynnaLab and oracles-disasm were created by [Stewmat](https://github.com/Stewmath/) (me) in a very dark cave while subsisting off of beaver tails and maple sap. Development began in 2015 and has proceeded since then like a sputtering faucet of code spewing out updates at completely unpredictable intervals.

Special thanks to:

  * [Lin](https://github.com/lin20) for creating ZOLE, the original Zelda Oracles Level Editor.
  * [Daniel Jianoran](https://github.com/vinheim3) for his work on the Oracle of Seasons part of oracles-disasm.
  * Beware for creating [BGB](https://bgb.bircd.org), in which countless hours were spent debugging functions and staring at numbers until my dreams consisted of Z80 opcodes floating in a void, taunting me, telling me I could never decode them. Well, I showed those opcodes who had the last laugh, didn't I???
  * [Ville Helin](https://github.com/vhelin/wla-dx) for WLA-DX, the assembler used by oracles-disasm.
  * Everyone on our [discord server](https://discord.gg/wCpPPNZ), for making cool stuff with LynnaLab and giving it a purpose. :-)
